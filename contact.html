⚠️ Medical Emergency & Patient Notice

Knockout Notes is an educational platform designed strictly for post-graduate medical revision and study. We do not provide clinical consultations, individual patient management advice, diagnostic opinions, or emergency medical services. For medical emergencies, consult local emergency services and institutional rapid response protocols immediately.

3. Communication Guidelines

  • Privacy Protection: Please do not send sensitive personal health information (PHI) or identifiable patient case details in your correspondence.
  • Expected Response Time: We endeavor to review all inquiries within 24 to 48 business hours (Monday through Friday).
  • Spam Safeguard: Unsolicited commercial advertisements, spam, automated SEO pitches, or promotional solicitations will be permanently blocked.
