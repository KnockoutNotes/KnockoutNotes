1. Overview & Nature of Products

Knockout Notes is an educational web platform dedicated to anaesthesia, critical care medicine, and post-graduate viva examination revision. All resources, knowledge bases, interactive clinical calculators, drug monographs, and visual learning aids provided on this website are strictly digital.

Where downloadable educational products (such as structured PowerPoint presentations, clinical summary notes, or digital revision guides) are offered now or in the future, all such items are fulfilled exclusively via electronic means.

ℹ️ No Physical Shipping

Knockout Notes does not sell, package, or dispatch any tangible physical goods. No physical shipments via postal mail, parcel services, or couriers are conducted, and no physical shipping or freight fees are ever charged.

2. Delivery Method & Timelines

2.1 Free Educational Resources

All core clinical resources currently accessible across Knockout Notes—including our evidence-based clinical calculators, clinical pearls, drug library monographs, 3D workstation explorer, and resuscitation algorithms—are delivered immediately and interactively in your web browser with zero wait time.

2.2 Downloadable Digital Products (When Applicable)

If you purchase or access an eligible digital educational product (such as a downloadable PDF study deck or presentation file):

  • Instant Browser Access: A direct download link is rendered on your screen immediately following successful verification of your transaction.
  • Electronic Mail Delivery: A confirmation email containing the secure download link and purchase receipt is typically dispatched to your registered email address within 5 to 15 minutes of completed payment.

3. Non-Receipt of Digital Access & Troubleshooting

If you have completed an authorized transaction for a digital educational resource but have not received your access link within 30 minutes, please follow these troubleshooting steps:

  1. Check Spam & Promotions Folders: Automated delivery emails may occasionally be routed to your email provider's "Spam", "Junk", or "Promotions" folders.
  2. Verify Email Accuracy: Ensure that the email address provided during checkout was typed accurately without typos.
  3. Network & Download Restrictions: Institutional hospital or university networks sometimes block external file downloads. Try accessing your download link on a personal connection or browser.

If you still cannot access your digital resource, please contact our support team immediately. We will verify your transaction and re-issue your download link within 24 to 48 business hours.

4. Current Service Notice

The core clinical modules of Knockout Notes are currently provided free of charge for medical educational use. This shipping policy transparently defines how digital distribution operates and governs any future digital products or downloadable additions launched on the platform.
