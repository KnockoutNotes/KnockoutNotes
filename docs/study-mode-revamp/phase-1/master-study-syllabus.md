## Anaesthesia – Proposed Major Categories
| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| General Anaesthesia | Fundamentals of induction, maintenance, emergence, equipment, pharmacology. |
| Airway & Respiratory | Airway assessment, devices, difficult airway algorithms, ventilation strategies. |
| Regional Anaesthesia | Spinal, epidural, peripheral blocks, anatomy, complications. |
| Cardiovascular Anaesthesia | Hemodynamic monitoring, cardiac surgery anaesthesia, valve management. |
| Neuroanaesthesia | Brain protection, intracranial pressure management, monitoring. |
| Obstetric Anaesthesia | Labour analgesia, maternal/fetal physiology, emergency C‑section. |
| Pediatric Anaesthesia | Age‑specific considerations, dosing, airway, equipment. |
| Speciality Anaesthesia | Burn, transplant, trauma, ENT, ophthalmic anaesthesia. |
| Critical Events & Peri‑operative Safety | Crises, checklists, WHO safe surgery, rapid response. |
| Monitoring & Investigations | Intra‑operative monitoring, TEE, arterial lines, labs. |
| Anaesthesia Equipment | Machines, circuits, vaporizers, safety systems. |
| Pain Medicine | Acute/chronic pain management, multimodal analgesia. |

## Critical Care – 16 Core Topics (Horizontal Navigation)
1. **General Principles of Critical Care** – Organisation, staffing, scoring systems, ethics, communication, QA.
2. **Airway Management** – Anatomy, predictors, guidelines, devices, RSI, post‑intubation, extubation.
3. **Respiratory Critical Care** – Physiology, ARDS, ventilation modes, weaning, NIV, HFNO, VAE prevention.
4. **Hemodynamic Support** – Shock classification, monitoring, vasopressors, inotropes, fluids, advanced monitoring.
5. **Sepsis & Infectious Diseases** – Definitions, bundles, resuscitation, antibiotics, stewardship.
6. **Neurological Critical Care** – coma assessment, GCS, ICP, TBI, status epilepticus, stroke, neuromuscular.
7. **Cardiovascular Critical Care** – ACS, arrhythmias, HF, shock, post‑arrest, myocarditis, tamponade.
8. **Renal & Metabolic Support** – AKI, RRT modalities, electrolytes, acid‑base, fluid balance, diuretics.
9. **Gastrointestinal & Hepatic Critical Care** – GI bleeding, pancreatitis, hepatic failure, nutrition, refeeding.
10. **Trauma & Burns** – ATLS, TBI, spinal injury, polytrauma, burn management, Parkland formula.
11. **Poisoning & Environmental Emergencies** – Common toxics, antidotes, heat stroke, hypothermia, drowning.
12. **Hematology & Transfusion** – Triggers, component therapy, DIC, massive transfusion, protocols.
13. **Obstetric Critical Care** – Eclampsia, HELLP, haemorrhage, shock index, amniotic fluid embolism.
14. **Pediatric Critical Care Essentials** – Physiology, airway, ventilation, shock, dosing.
15. **ICU Pharmacology** – Sedation, analgesia, neuromuscular blockers, drug dosing in organ failure.
16. **Research & Recent Advances** – ECMO, biomarkers, ventilation innovations, AI, guideline updates.

## Drugs – Proposed Major Categories
| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Induction Agents | IV hypnotics for induction (propofol, etomidate, ketamine). |
| Sedatives | Non‑induction sedatives (midazolam, dexmedetomidine). |
| Opioids | Analgesic opioids (fentanyl, remifentanil, morphine). |
| Non‑opioid Analgesics | NSAIDs, acetaminophen, gabapentinoids. |
| Neuromuscular Blockers | Depolarising & non‑depolarising agents. |
| Reversal Agents | Sugammadex, neostigmine, atropine. |
| Local Anaesthetics | Amide & ester agents for regional blocks. |
| Inhalational Agents | Volatile anaesthetics (sevoflurane, isoflurane). |
| Vasopressors & Inotropes | Catecholamines, vasopressin, milrinone. |
| Cardiovascular Drugs | Anti‑arrhythmics, anti‑coagulants, anticoagulation reversal. |
| ICU Drugs | Sedatives, analgesics, electrolytes, insulin protocols. |
| Emergency Drugs | Adrenaline, atropine, calcium gluconate, lipid rescue. |
| Obstetric & Pediatric Drugs | Uterotonics, neonatal analgesics, dosing considerations. |

*The table above reflects the intended high‑level taxonomy. Existing content will be mapped to these categories in the migration matrix.*
