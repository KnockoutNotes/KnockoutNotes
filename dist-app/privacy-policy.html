1. Introduction

Knockout Notes ("we", "us", or "our") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, and safeguarded when you visit our educational website, use our clinical calculators, or communicate with us.

2. What Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Voluntarily Provide

We collect personal information that you intentionally communicate to us, including:

  • Email Correspondence: When you send us an email query, we receive your email address, sender name, and the text or attachments included in your message.
  • Voluntary Contributions & Support: When you support Knockout Notes via our public contribution link, your transaction is processed by an authorized third-party payment gateway. We receive confirmation records (such as transaction IDs, contribution amounts, date/time, and payer contact details supplied to the gateway), but we never receive your financial account credentials.

2.2 Technical & Hosting Logs

When you access our website, our hosting network (Cloudflare) automatically records standard web server log entries necessary for routing, security, and DDoS protection. These logs may contain your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, device operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, and access timestamps.

2.3 Local Client-Side Storage (No Third-Party Ad Cookies)

Knockout Notes does not use commercial cross-site advertising or tracking cookies. We utilize your web browser's local storage (LocalStorage) strictly for local device functionality:

  • kn-theme: Remembers whether you have selected Dark Mode or Light Mode across visits.
  • Progressive Web App (PWA) Cache: Stores static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and 3D assets on your device via a Service Worker so the site loads quickly and functions reliably on clinical hospital Wi-Fi networks.

3. Payment Processing & Critical Transparency

💳 Third-Party Payment Gateway Details

Payments and voluntary contributions on Knockout Notes are processed externally by Razorpay.

Payments are not anonymous. In compliance with banking regulations and Know Your Customer (KYC) standards, the payment gateway processes the payer's identity, contact information, and payment method details. The gateway may also display the account holder's registered business or legal name as on file with the payment provider. Razorpay manages all transaction information according to its own privacy policy. Knockout Notes does not collect, view, or retain your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs.

4. How We Use Collected Information

Information collected is utilized exclusively for legitimate educational and operational purposes:

  • To respond to your feedback, support requests, and content suggestions.
  • To investigate and resolve reported bugs, calculation inconsistencies, or broken links.
  • To verify and confirm voluntary contribution transactions or digital product access.
  • To protect the security, integrity, and operational availability of the website.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with trusted service providers necessary to operate the platform:

  • Infrastructure & Security Providers: Cloudflare (hosting infrastructure, CDN routing, edge caching, and DDoS mitigation).
  • Payment Service Providers: Razorpay (payment processing and settlement when you initiate a voluntary contribution).
  • Legal Compliance: If required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority with competent jurisdiction.

6. Data Security & Storage

All traffic between your browser and Knockout Notes is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS). We maintain security practices to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

We retain support emails and transaction confirmation records only for as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry, maintain accurate accounting records, or comply with legal obligations, after which they are securely deleted.

8. User Rights & Privacy Requests

You may request information regarding any personal correspondence data we hold related to your email address, or request that your support emails be purged from our records. To exercise these rights, email us at knockoutnotes.anaesthesia@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Knockout Notes is a specialized medical education platform intended for healthcare practitioners and post-graduate medical trainees. It is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our platform or legal standards. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
