1. Purpose & Scope

This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines the terms and procedures governing voluntary support contributions and any digital educational products offered on Knockout Notes ("we", "us", or "our"). Our aim is to ensure fair, transparent, and prompt resolution of payment inquiries.

2. Voluntary Peer Support ("Buy Me a Coffee")

Knockout Notes offers an optional "Buy Me a Coffee" feature that allows users to make voluntary micro-contributions to assist with ongoing web hosting, 3D medical graphics modeling, and content curation costs.

2.1 General Rule for Contributions

Because voluntary contributions are initiated at the supporter's sole discretion without an exchange for commercial goods or services, and funds are immediately allocated to platform maintenance, voluntary contributions are generally non-refundable once successfully processed.

2.2 Exceptions for Contributions

We will readily evaluate and issue refunds for voluntary contributions under the following circumstances:

  • Accidental Duplicate Charges: If a technical glitch or multiple accidental clicks resulted in multiple identical deductions for the same contribution within a 24-hour period.
  • Erroneous Transaction Amount: If a demonstrably incorrect amount was entered and reported promptly within 48 hours of the transaction.
  • Unauthorized / Fraudulent Usage: If the transaction is confirmed to have been initiated through unauthorized card use or validated fraud.
ℹ️ Charitable Status Notice

Knockout Notes is an independent educational initiative and is not a registered charitable trust, NGO, or tax-exempt non-profit organization under Section 80G. Voluntary contributions are peer-to-peer educational support and cannot be claimed as tax-deductible charitable donations.

3. Digital Educational Monographs & Revision Guides

Digital chapter monographs and revision guides on Knockout Notes are delivered instantaneously in PDF format upon verified payment settlement. Because digital resources are non-tangible and accessible perpetually from your personal Workspace upon confirmation, purchases are generally final once the digital monograph is unlocked. However, full refunds or corrected access are granted under the following verified conditions:

  • Persistent Technical Delivery Failure: If payment cleared successfully on Cashfree but the server-side monograph failed to generate or unlock, and our technical support team cannot provide working access within 48 hours of notification.
  • Accidental Duplicate Purchases: If a technical glitch or double-click resulted in multiple identical transactions for the same chapter on the same account within a 7-day window.
  • Corrupted File Transmission: If the generated monograph PDF is corrupted or incomplete and our system cannot compile a corrected copy.

4. Order Cancellations & Pending Transactions

Transactions initiated through Cashfree may occasionally remain in a Pending state due to inter-bank reconciliation delays:

  • Pending Order Cancellation: Learners may cancel an unverified pending order request directly from the Workspace "Payment History & Orders" tab.
  • Bank Debit Protection: If money was already debited from your bank account or UPI app, our system performs an authoritative live status check before cancellation. If the payment was captured by the bank, your monograph access is granted immediately. If not captured, you may lodge a dispute through our on-site desk.

5. How to Submit a Dispute or Refund Request

We provide two direct channels to lodge a payment inquiry or refund request:

  1. On-Site Dispute Desk (Recommended): Navigate to your Workspace profile $\rightarrow$ "Payment History & Orders" $\rightarrow$ click "⚠️ Issue" on the relevant order, or "⚠️ Report a Payment Issue". This automatically populates your Order ID and sends an instant priority alert to our administrative desk.
  2. Direct Email Escalation: Email our support desk directly at knockoutnotes.anaesthesia@gmail.com within 7 calendar days of the transaction with:
    • Learner name and registered KnockoutNotes account email.
    • Order Reference ID (e.g. order_kn_...) or Cashfree Reference / Bank UTR.
    • Transaction date and amount in INR.
    • Clear description of the issue (e.g., amount debited but order stayed pending, duplicate charge).

6. Refund Processing & Bank Timelines

  • Review & Investigation: Our administrative team will verify the gateway transaction logs with Cashfree and respond within 1 to 2 business days.
  • Disbursement Method: Approved refunds are issued strictly back to the original payment method (UPI VPA, bank account, or debit/credit card) through Cashfree. We do not issue cash refunds or transfer funds to unverified third-party accounts.
  • Banking Settlement: Once initiated via Cashfree, standard banking processing typically reflects in your bank account within 5 to 7 business days (depending on your bank's NEFT/IMPS/UPI settlement cycles).
