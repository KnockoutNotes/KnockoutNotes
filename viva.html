Viva #001

Why does N₂O expand a closed gas space?

N₂O diffuses into closed gas spaces faster than nitrogen leaves, increasing gas volume and/or pressure. This is why N₂O is avoided when expansion of a closed gas space would be harmful.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Barash, Cullen & Stoelting's Clinical Anesthesia, 9th ed.
Viva #002

What is the formula for CPP?

CPP = MAP − ICP

When CVP exceeds ICP, the effective downstream pressure may instead be represented by CVP in the relevant clinical setting.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Barash, Cullen & Stoelting's Clinical Anesthesia, 9th ed.
Viva #003

Why is etomidate relatively haemodynamically stable?

Compared with propofol, etomidate generally produces less cardiovascular depression and tends to preserve sympathetic tone and baroreflex function better.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Stoelting 6th ed.
Viva #004

Shunt vs dead space?

Shunt: perfusion without adequate ventilation.
Dead space: ventilation without adequate perfusion.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; West's Respiratory Physiology.
Viva #005

What happens to MAC with age?

MAC generally decreases with increasing age. The age effect is clinically important when titrating inhaled anaesthetics.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Stoelting 6th ed.
Viva #006

Why does spinal anaesthesia cause hypotension?

Sympathetic blockade causes arterial and venous vasodilation, reducing systemic vascular resistance and venous return; cardiac output may fall, depending on the level of block and compensatory mechanisms.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Barash, Cullen & Stoelting's Clinical Anesthesia, 9th ed.
