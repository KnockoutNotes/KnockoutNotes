1. Purpose & Scope

This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines the terms and procedures governing voluntary support contributions and any digital educational products offered on Knockout Notes ("we", "us", or "our"). Our aim is to ensure fair, transparent, and prompt resolution of payment inquiries.

2. Voluntary Peer Support ("Buy Me a Coffee")

Knockout Notes offers an optional "Buy Me a Coffee" feature that allows users to make voluntary micro-contributions to assist with ongoing web hosting, 3D medical graphics modeling, and content curation costs.

2.1 General Rule for Contributions

Because voluntary contributions are initiated at the supporter's sole discretion without an exchange for commercial goods or services, and funds are immediately allocated to platform maintenance, voluntary contributions are generally non-refundable once successfully processed.

2.2 Exceptions for Contributions

We will readily evaluate and issue refunds for voluntary contributions under the following circumstances:

  • Accidental Duplicate Charges: If a technical glitch or multiple accidental clicks resulted in multiple identical deductions for the same contribution within a 24-hour period.
  • Erroneous Transaction Amount: If a demonstrably incorrect amount was entered and reported promptly within 48 hours of the transaction.
  • Unauthorized / Fraudulent Usage: If the transaction is confirmed to have been initiated through unauthorized card use or validated fraud.
ℹ️ Charitable Status Notice

Knockout Notes is an independent educational initiative and is not a registered charitable trust, NGO, or tax-exempt non-profit organization under Section 80G. Voluntary contributions are peer-to-peer educational support and cannot be claimed as tax-deductible charitable donations.

3. Downloadable Digital Products (When Applicable)

If you purchase an eligible standalone digital educational product (such as a downloadable PowerPoint presentation, comprehensive revision deck, or summary document) when officially launched on the platform:

3.1 Digital Product Refund Terms

Due to the non-tangible, irrevocable nature of digital downloads, completed purchases are generally final once the digital file has been accessed or downloaded. However, you are entitled to a full refund or immediate replacement access in the following verified cases:

  • Persistent Technical Delivery Failure: If you did not receive a functional download link and our technical support team is unable to provide working file access within 48 hours of your support request.
  • Corrupted or Defective Files: If the downloaded file is corrupted, unreadable, or missing core content as advertised, and we cannot furnish a corrected replacement within 48 hours.
  • Duplicate Purchase: Accidental repeated purchase of the exact same digital asset on the same account within a 7-day window.

4. Cancellation of Orders

Because digital access and downloadable resources are fulfilled automatically and immediately upon payment confirmation (as detailed in our Shipping & Delivery Policy), orders for digital goods cannot be canceled once the payment has cleared and the digital link has been generated.

5. How to Submit a Refund or Payment Query

To request a refund or raise an inquiry regarding an erroneous transaction, please follow this procedure:

  1. Send an email to our designated support desk at: knockoutnotes.anaesthesia@gmail.com
  2. Submit your request within 7 calendar days of the transaction date.
  3. Include the following details in your email to expedite verification:
    • Your full name (as provided to the payment gateway).
    • Registered email address used during payment.
    • Payment Gateway Transaction Reference ID / Order ID (e.g. Razorpay Payment ID starting with pay_).
    • Date and amount of the transaction.
    • A concise description of the reason for the refund request (e.g., duplicate charge, technical non-delivery).

6. Refund Processing & Timelines

  • Review Window: Our team will review your submission and notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund within 2 to 3 business days.
  • Disbursement Method: Approved refunds are credited exclusively back to the original payment method (credit card, debit card, net banking, or UPI account) used during the initial transaction via the payment gateway. We do not issue cash refunds or transfers to alternate third-party accounts.
  • Bank Credit Timeline: Once processed by us, payment gateway and banking settlement typically takes 5 to 7 business days to reflect in your bank account or card statement, depending on your financial institution.

7. Payment Support Contact

For all payment disputes, receipt requests, or cancellation inquiries, contact our brand support team:
