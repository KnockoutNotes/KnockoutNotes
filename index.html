Pharmacology

Succinylcholine — IV Intubating Dose

For adult tracheal intubation, standard rapid sequence induction dose is commonly cited as 1–1.5 mg/kg IV.

Dose: 1.0 – 1.5 mg/kg IV
Viva point: Rapid onset (30–60s) follows rapid access to neuromuscular junction nicotinic acetylcholine receptors, producing initial phase I depolarisation block. Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Stoelting's Pharmacology & Physiology, 6th ed.

🧠 Viva • Today's Question
Explore All →

Why does N₂O expand a closed gas space?

A classic exam question testing differential diffusion coefficients across body cavities.

N₂O is 34 times more soluble in blood than nitrogen. It diffuses into closed gas cavities far faster than nitrogen leaves, causing rapid expansion of volume or pressure. Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Barash 9th ed.

🚨 Critical Care
ICU Ventilation Protocol

Low Tidal Volume Strategy in ARDS

Lung-protective ventilation target based on predicted body weight (PBW).

Target: 4 – 8 mL/kg PBW
Target 4–8 mL/kg predicted body weight, maintain plateau pressure ≤ 30 cmH₂O, and titrate PEEP according to driving pressure goals (ΔP ≤ 14 cmH₂O). Reference: Surviving Sepsis & ARDS Guidelines 2026.
