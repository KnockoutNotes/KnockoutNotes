Pharmacology

Succinylcholine — IV intubating dose

For adult tracheal intubation, standard rapid sequence induction dose is commonly cited as 1–1.5 mg/kg IV.

Dose: 1.0 – 1.5 mg/kg IV
Viva point: Rapid onset follows rapid access to the neuromuscular junction and depolarizing activation of nicotinic acetylcholine receptors. Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Stoelting's Pharmacology & Physiology in Anesthetic Practice, 6th ed.

🧠 Viva Classics
Physics & Physiology

Why does N₂O expand a closed gas space?

A classic exam question testing diffusion physics across body cavities.

N₂O diffuses into closed gas spaces approximately 34 times faster than nitrogen leaves, causing rapid expansion of volume and/or pressure. Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Barash 9th ed.

ICU Ventilation

Low Tidal Volume Strategy in ARDS

Lung-protective ventilation target based on predicted body weight (PBW).

Target: 4 – 8 mL/kg PBW
A commonly used target is 4–8 mL/kg predicted body weight, adjusted according to plateau pressure (≤ 30 cmH₂O) and driving pressure goals. Reference: Surviving Sepsis & ARDS Guidelines 2026.
