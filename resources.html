Textbooks & Standards

KnockoutNotes strictly prioritises current international guidelines, clinical consensus statements, and standard post-graduate anaesthesia textbooks.

Gold Standard
Textbooks

Core Curriculum Textbooks

Current editions referenced across all content:

  • Miller's Anesthesia, 10th Edition
  • Barash, Cullen & Stoelting's Clinical Anesthesia, 9th Edition
  • Stoelting's Pharmacology & Physiology in Anesthetic Practice, 6th Edition
  • West's Respiratory Physiology: The Essentials, 11th Edition
Publisher citations and editions verified in 2026.
Resuscitation
International Guidelines

AHA & ERC Resuscitation Standards

All cardiac arrest algorithms, pediatric life support, and post-resuscitation care are aligned with the 2025 American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC and European Resuscitation Council standards.

2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR and ECC, Adult Basic & Advanced Life Support.

EDITORIAL INTEGRITY

Evidence Hierarchy Policy

Every clinical answer carries a verified source citation.

Policy Hierarchy
Evidence-Based

Order of Evidence Priority: Major clinical society guidelines and consensus statements (ASA, DAS, ESAIC, AHA, SCCM) take precedence for procedural algorithms and dosage recommendations. Core physiology and pharmacology mechanisms reference the standard textbooks above. In clinical practice, always verify current product labelling, institutional protocols, and patient-specific variables.

Editorial governance: KnockoutNotes Medical Education Board.
