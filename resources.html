Resuscitation
2025 Update

2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR & ECC

All cardiac arrest algorithms, pediatric life support, and post-resuscitation care strictly adhere to the 2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR and ECC:

  • Chest Compression Metrics: Rate 100–120/min, depth 5–6 cm (2–2.4 in), complete recoil, and chest compression fraction (CCF) > 80%.
  • Refractory Shockable Rhythms: Double sequential external defibrillation (DSED) and vector change (VC) defibrillation endorsed for persistent VF/pVT.
  • Waveform Capnography: Continuous quantitative EtCO2; values < 10 mmHg guide CPR compression optimization; sudden rise > 35–40 mmHg indicates ROSC.
  • Post-ROSC Care: Strict targeted temperature management (32°C–36°C or active fever prevention < 37.5°C), normoxia (SpO2 92–98%), and immediate coronary angiography.
2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR and ECC, Circulation Guideline Supplement.
Pulmonology & Airway
Global Strategy

GINA Asthma Strategy & Perioperative Care

Key evidence-based recommendations from the Global Initiative for Asthma (GINA) applied to perioperative care and acute airway management:

  • Reliever Paradigm: Anti-inflammatory reliever therapy (as-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol) preferred across all tracks; avoid SABA-only treatment to reduce severe exacerbations.
  • Preoperative Optimization: Defer elective surgery if active wheeze, recent systemic corticosteroid bursts, or FEV1 < 80%; prescribe 3–5 days of oral prednisolone (0.5–1 mg/kg/day) for suboptimal control.
  • Intraoperative Airway Strategy: Prioritise regional anesthesia; use volatile agents (sevoflurane) or ketamine for bronchodilation; avoid desflurane and airway instrumentation during light anaesthesia planes.
  • Acute Bronchospasm Protocol: High-dose nebulized SABA + ipratropium, IV magnesium sulphate (25–50 mg/kg, max 2 g), IV dexamethasone/hydrocortisone, and low-rate, prolonged expiratory time ventilation.
GINA Global Strategy for Asthma Management and Prevention (Updated Clinical Strategy).
Critical Care
Consensus Definition

New Global Definition of ARDS (ESICM Update)

Major international consensus update expanding the Berlin definition formulated by the European Society of Intensive Care Medicine (ESICM):

  • Inclusion of Non-Invasive Modalities: Recognizes ARDS in patients on High-Flow Nasal Cannula (HFNC ≥ 30 L/min) or continuous CPAP/NIV without requiring invasive endotracheal intubation.
  • SpO2/FiO2 Staging Index: Formal validation of SpO2/FiO2 ratio (≤ 315 when SpO2 ≤ 97%) as an accurate surrogate for PaO2/FiO2, ensuring rapid bedside diagnosis in resource-variable settings.
  • Point-of-Care Ultrasound (POCUS): Lung ultrasound demonstrating bilateral interstitial/alveolar syndromes (B-lines) formally accepted alongside chest X-ray and CT imaging.
  • Lung Protective Mechanical Ventilation: Strict low tidal volume (4–6 mL/kg PBW), driving pressure < 14 cmH2O, plateau pressure < 30 cmH2O, and early prone positioning (≥ 16 h/day) for severe hypoxaemia.
ESICM New Global Definition of ARDS, Intensive Care Medicine Consensus Statement.
