Clinical Pearls

Resuscitation

2025 AHA ALS

The current AHA adult advanced life support guideline set is the 2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR and ECC.

The 2025 ALS guidance covers adult cardiac arrest, peri-arrest arrhythmias, defibrillation, cardioversion, airway management and drug therapy.Reference: 2025 American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC.
Ventilation

Low tidal volume strategy

For ARDS, lung-protective ventilation is based on predicted body weight rather than actual body weight.

A commonly used ARDS target is 4–8 mL/kg predicted body weight, adjusted according to plateau pressure and gas exchange goals.
Monitoring

ETCO₂ suddenly disappears

Don't assume it is an equipment problem until the patient has been rapidly assessed.

Rapidly assess circulation, airway position/patency, circuit connections and sampling. Abrupt loss can accompany disconnection, oesophageal intubation, complete obstruction, cardiac arrest or massive reduction in pulmonary blood flow.
Shock

Shock framework

Classify the physiology before choosing the pressor or fluid.

Think distributive, hypovolaemic, cardiogenic and obstructive physiology; integrate examination, ultrasound where available, lactate trend and dynamic assessment of fluid responsiveness.
