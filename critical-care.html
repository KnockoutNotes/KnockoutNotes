Resuscitation
AHA Guidelines

2025 AHA ALS Guidelines

The standard adult advanced life support framework is the 2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR and ECC.

Covers adult cardiac arrest, peri-arrest tachy- and brady-arrhythmias, high-energy defibrillation, early airway intervention, and targeted temperature management post-ROSC. Reference: 2025 American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC.
Ventilation
ARDS Management

Low Tidal Volume Strategy in ARDS

Lung-protective ventilation calculated on predicted body weight, not actual body weight.

Target: 4 – 8 mL/kg PBW
Calculate PBW from patient height and sex. Target tidal volume 4–8 mL/kg PBW, maintain plateau pressure ≤ 30 cmH₂O, titrate PEEP according to FiO₂ tables or driving pressure (ΔP ≤ 14 cmH₂O). Reference: ARDSNet Protocols; Surviving Sepsis Guidelines 2026.
Monitoring
Emergency

ETCO₂ Suddenly Disappears in the ICU

Never assume monitor failure before patient safety is confirmed.

Immediately palpate central pulse and confirm chest rise. Differential: Disconnection of ventilator circuit, accidental extubation/oesophageal displacement, total airway obstruction (mucus plug), massive pulmonary embolism, or abrupt cardiac arrest. Reference: Principles of Critical Care, 5th ed.
Haemodynamics
Physiological Classification

Shock Framework

Classify hemodynamic physiology before selecting vasopressors or inotropes.

Four primary phenotypes: Distributive (low SVR, high/normal CO), Hypovolaemic (low preload, high SVR), Cardiogenic (low CO, elevated filling pressures), and Obstructive (tamponade, tension pneumothorax, massive PE). Use bedside ultrasound and dynamic fluid responsiveness measures. Reference: The ICU Book, Paul Marino, 5th ed.
