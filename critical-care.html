Resuscitation

2025 AHA ALS

The current AHA adult advanced life support guideline set is the 2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR and ECC.

The 2025 ALS guidance covers adult cardiac arrest, peri-arrest arrhythmias, defibrillation, cardioversion, airway management and drug therapy. Use the current official algorithm at the bedside rather than an old screenshot.Reference: 2025 American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC, Part 9: Adult Advanced Life Support.
Ventilation

Low tidal volume strategy

For ARDS, lung-protective ventilation is based on predicted body weight rather than actual body weight.

A commonly used ARDS target is 4–8 mL/kg predicted body weight, with adjustment according to plateau pressure and gas exchange goals.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; current ARDS guideline/consensus documents.
Monitoring

ETCO₂ suddenly disappears

Don't assume it is an equipment problem until the patient has been rapidly assessed.

Rapidly assess circulation, airway position/patency, circuit connections and sampling. Abrupt loss can accompany disconnection, oesophageal intubation, complete obstruction, cardiac arrest or massive reduction in pulmonary blood flow.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Barash, Cullen & Stoelting's Clinical Anesthesia, 9th ed.
Shock

Shock framework

Classify the physiology before choosing the pressor or fluid.

Think distributive, hypovolaemic, cardiogenic and obstructive physiology; then integrate clinical examination, echocardiography/ultrasound where available, lactate trend and dynamic assessment of fluid responsiveness.Reference: Barash, Cullen & Stoelting's Clinical Anesthesia, 9th ed.; current critical-care guidelines.
