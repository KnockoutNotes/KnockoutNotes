Cardiovascular

Mitral Stenosis

Reduced mitral valve area creates a diastolic inflow obstruction and raises left atrial pressure.

Anaesthetic priorities:
  • Avoid tachycardia.
  • Preserve sinus rhythm where possible.
  • Maintain adequate preload without fluid overload.
  • Avoid acute increases in PVR.
Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Barash, Cullen & Stoelting's Clinical Anesthesia, 9th ed.
Respiratory

V/Q mismatch

Uneven matching of ventilation and perfusion is a major mechanism of hypoxaemia.

Low V/Q: perfusion exceeds ventilation. High V/Q: ventilation exceeds perfusion. A true shunt is perfusion of non-ventilated alveoli.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; West's Respiratory Physiology: The Essentials, current edition.
Airway

Preoxygenation

Its purpose is to maximise oxygen stores before induction and apnoea.

Core principle: replace alveolar nitrogen with oxygen and increase the oxygen fraction of the functional residual capacity.Reference: Miller's Anesthesia, 10th ed.; Barash, Cullen & Stoelting's Clinical Anesthesia, 9th ed.
