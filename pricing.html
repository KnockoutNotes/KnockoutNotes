2. Current Pricing Status: 100% Free Core Access

At present, all clinical knowledge and calculation tools on Knockout Notes are provided free of charge. There are no mandatory subscription plans, hidden paywalls, timed trial locks, or gated registrations required to use the website.

Because no paid digital products are actively sold on the website today, no commercial prices are listed for the core modules. If specific standalone digital products (such as curated slide decks or downloadable question sets) are launched in the future, their exact one-time prices, file formats, and licensing terms will be clearly displayed prior to checkout.

3. Payment Processing & Taxes

All online payments and voluntary contributions are routed through authorized, compliant third-party payment gateways (such as Razorpay):

  • Currency: Standard transactions are billed in Indian Rupees (INR) or the payer's local currency equivalent via international card processing where supported.
  • Applicable Taxes & Levies: If commercial digital products are sold in the future, all applicable statutory taxes (such as Goods and Services Tax / GST) and payment processing fees will be itemized clearly on the payment screen prior to completion.
  • Secure Transmission: Payment card details, bank credentials, and UPI authorizations are processed directly by the payment gateway over encrypted SSL/TLS connections and are never seen or stored on Knockout Notes servers.
