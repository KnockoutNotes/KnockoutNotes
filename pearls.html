Pharmacology

Succinylcholine

IV intubating dose: 1–1.5 mg/kg in adults.

Depolarizing NMBA with rapid onset. It produces fasciculations followed by flaccid paralysis.
Pharmacology

Sugammadex

Dose according to depth of rocuronium/vecuronium block.

2 mg/kg for moderate block; 4 mg/kg for deep block; 16 mg/kg for immediate reversal after a 1.2 mg/kg rocuronium dose in the relevant setting.
Physiology

FRC as an oxygen reservoir

Preoxygenation replaces alveolar nitrogen with oxygen and increases oxygen reserve during apnoea.

The goal is denitrogenation and maximisation of oxygen stores, not merely achieving an SpO₂ of 100%.
Airway

Can't intubate, can't oxygenate

Recognise failure early and move to the rescue pathway.

Priorities are oxygenation, declaration of the emergency, optimisation of rescue attempts and progression through the applicable difficult-airway algorithm.
Cardiac

Mitral stenosis: rate matters

Tachycardia shortens diastole and reduces LV filling across a stenotic mitral valve.

Aim for controlled heart rate, sinus rhythm when possible, adequate but not excessive preload, and avoidance of acute rises in PVR.
Critical Care

Sudden loss of ETCO₂

Think patient, airway, circuit and circulation immediately.

Consider disconnection, airway obstruction, oesophageal intubation, sampling/circuit failure, pulmonary embolism and abrupt reduction in pulmonary blood flow.
